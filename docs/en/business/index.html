OUR BUSINESS What We Do

Resource trading at our core,
with operations across several fields.

We purchase fluorspar concentrate from mining companies in Mongolia, verify its quality, arrange export, and deliver it to customers in Japan. That is our business.

Import & Export of Mineral Resources

We supply and export Mongolian fluorspar and raw materials to overseas markets, starting with Japan.

International Trade

Beyond mineral resources, we run a broader trading business connecting Mongolia with the world.

Investment & Consulting

We invest in resource ventures and provide consulting for business development in Mongolia.

Other Ventures

Energy BusinessFuture

We aim to expand into energy resources such as crude oil, petroleum products, and natural gas.

HOW WE WORK Our Process

From Mongolian mines,
to our customers in Japan.

We are a resource trading company: we purchase fluorspar concentrate from mining companies in Mongolia, verify its quality, arrange export, and deliver it to customers in Japan.

  1. MONGOLIAN MINES

    Sourcing

    Partner mining companies in Mongolia produce the fluorspar concentrate.

  2. PURCHASE & QC

    Purchase & Quality Check

    We purchase the concentrate and rigorously verify its composition, grade, and particle size.

  3. ALTAN KHULEG

    Export Logistics

    We arrange export customs clearance and shipping, responsibly.

  4. SALE

    Sale

    We sell to our customers in Japan under agreed contracts.

  5. JAPANESE CUSTOMERS

    Japanese Customers

    Fluorspar concentrate is delivered to customers across Japan.

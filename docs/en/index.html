ABOUT US Company Profile

Connecting Japan and Mongolia
as a resource trading company.

ALTAN KHULEG RESOURCES LLC is a resource trading company established through joint investment by Japan and Mongolia.

We import and sell concentrate produced at fluorspar mines in Mongolia to customers in Japan. As a trusted partner, we aim to create long-term value for both countries.
